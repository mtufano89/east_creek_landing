📋 Reservation Process

  1. Check the calendar for available dates
  2. Contact us by phone or email
  3. Schedule a tour (optional but recommended)
  4. Submit deposit to reserve your date
  5. Finalize details closer to your event

⏰ Booking Timeline

Popular dates (weekends, holidays) book 6-12 months in advance.

Weekday events often have more flexibility and shorter lead times.

Last-minute availability? Call us! We sometimes have openings for events within 30 days.

💡 Planning Tips

  • Saturday evenings are our most popular time slots
  • Friday and Sunday events often have better availability
  • Off-season months (Jan-Mar) tend to have more openings
  • Bingo is held every Monday evening
